Tharsos

noun | Greek: θάρσος

Courage, confidence, boldness. A holy audacity.

Tharsos was founded in 2026 by two young Catholics who recognized a profound gap between the reverence of the Mass and the everyday garments of the faithful. We founded this brand to enable likeminded young men and women to wear their faith—not as a performative gesture, but as a quiet, daily commitment.

We believe that what you wear on the outside should reflect the profound reality of the soul within. Our garments are designed to endure, grounded in tradition and built for the modern world. We try to equip young Catholics with:

01

Profession of Faith

Real Catholic merchandise. Not feel-good quotes or trendy slogans, but a bold profession of faith that one can wear with dignity and conviction in the public square.

02

Radical Charity

Ten percent of our profit is given without exception to pro-life charities. We support the organizations that house expectant mothers and defend the unborn when it costs something.

03

Uncompromising Craft

Heavyweight cottons, honest wools, and cuts that hold their shape. We reject fast fashion in favor of garments made to last decades, reflecting the eternal truths we stand for.

04

The Church Militant

We exist to foster a brotherhood of young Catholic men supporting one another. When you wear Tharsos, you stand shoulder-to-shoulder with others fighting the good fight.
