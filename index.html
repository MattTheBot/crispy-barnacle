Tharsos

noun | Greek: θάρσος

Courage, confidence, boldness. A holy audacity.

Tharsos started in 2026 with a simple frustration: the clothes we wore didn't match the faith we lived. As two young Catholics, we wanted garments that felt substantial, looked sharp, and spoke to our beliefs without shouting.

We built this brand for the men and women who take their faith seriously and want to carry it into the world with quiet confidence. We aren't here to sell you feel-good slogans. We're here to offer a real profession of faith you can actually wear. Read our full mission.
